History

From Vine star, to hit artist, songwriter and actor. Shawn Mendes started his career at the age of 13 when he shared his passion of music by publishing musical covers on Vine which got him noticed by the music industry. Debuting in 2014 with his single "Life of the Party" he made his mark in the industry, following with a debut album the next year. Many albums and many years later, he is loved by many — even getting his own documentary and starring in movies and tv-shows alongside winning awards and pulling in 28 billion streams.
